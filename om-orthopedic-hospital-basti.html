About Om Orthopedic Hospital

Bone, joint, spine and trauma care for Basti, in one building.

Om Orthopedic & Multi Speciality Hospital is on Pachpedia Road, Dadwa, Patel Chowk. Fracture surgery, joint replacement, arthroscopy, spine and pain care, and orthopaedic physiotherapy are all available here, and emergency services stay open day and night.

The hospital is led by Director Dr. Nidhi Gupta. Dr. D. K. Gupta, MBBS, MS (Orthopaedics), is its patron and senior orthopaedic surgeon.

Your care, start to finish

Surgery, imaging and physiotherapy are in the same building, so you do not need to travel between clinics.

  1. Arrive

    Emergency is open every hour. For appointments, book ahead on WhatsApp.

  2. Diagnose

    Examination and imaging to find the exact injury or joint problem.

  3. Treat

    Surgery in our operation theatre, or non-surgical care where that is enough.

  4. Recover

    Orthopaedic physiotherapy to get you walking and working again.
